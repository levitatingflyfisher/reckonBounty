The loop

How it works

Four message kinds, all plain JSON files your client can move over anything — share sheet, chat, USB, carrier pigeon. No accounts anywhere.

  1. Ask

    Write the decision as a binary, multi-choice, or quantity question. It is de-identified on your device and stamped with a privacy tier before it goes anywhere.

  2. Answer

    Independent bots reply with a probability, a rationale, base rates, and the clarifying questions they wish they could ask. Each discloses its model and method.

  3. Decide

    The forecasts are advice, not orders. You decide; the decision never leaves your hands.

  4. Score

    Record what actually happened. Brier and log scores update every advisor’s track record — computed on your device, identical in any client.

Spec §1 · invariants I1–I6

Why this is not a betting market

You can never win money here. You can only buy advice.

Personal questions can’t be two-sided markets: the asker is the oracle — only you can say whether buying the cabin turned out to be the right call — so anyone betting against you would have to trust your self-report. reckonBounty inverts the shape. The asker pays for a service, never stakes on an outcome, and six invariants keep it that way:

  • I1

    No market.

    No positions, no counterparties, no order book. Nobody but the asker ever pays in, and nobody can take out more than the asker chose to pay for the service.

  • I2

    The asker funds everything.

    Because the pot is 100% asker-funded, a dishonest resolution can only misallocate the asker’s own money. Nobody can be extracted from.

  • I3

    No custody.

    No custodian, no operator holding funds. When payments exist, they flow directly from asker to bot — or through an autonomous contract with no administrative keys.

  • I4

    The asker is the oracle.

    Resolution is self-reported. Scoring therefore exists for reputation, never for wagering.

  • I5

    Privacy is a first-class field.

    Every request declares a privacy tier; every bot declares which tiers it serves.

  • I6

    The ledger lives with you.

    Track records are computed client-side from stored request/response/resolution triples. No central server is required for the protocol to work.

Spec §5 · privacy tiers

Who sees your question is a field, not a promise

Every request declares one of four tiers. Redaction happens on your device, before anything leaves it.

TierWhat it meansStatus
public The question may be shared anywhere, as-is. live · v0
redacted De-identified on your device — by a local model or your own edit — before it leaves. Bots must not attempt re-identification. live · v0
attested Sent only to endpoints presenting a valid confidential-compute attestation. Attestation format reserved for v1. reserved
fhe Fully homomorphic evaluation. Plaintext-scale LLM inference under FHE is still 3–4 orders of magnitude away; the tier exists so the enum never breaks. reserved

Two tiers are live; two are named now so the wire format is ready before the technology is.

Quickstart · transport = files

Run a bot in 5 minutes

Python ≥ 3.11, zero runtime dependencies. The reference bot is rb answer: echo is the deterministic offline backend; swap in claude-cli or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (openai:<base_url> — llamafile, Ollama) when you want a real model.

git clone https://github.com/levitatingflyfisher/reckonBounty
cd reckonBounty
pip install -e tools/

# ask → answer → resolve → score: the whole protocol, offline
rb ask --title "Buy the vacation cabin?" > request.json
rb answer request.json --backend echo > response.json
rb resolve request.json --outcome no > resolution.json
rb score request.json resolution.json response.json

Or settle the shipped duel right now — the same one stamped on the slip above:

rb validate examples/cabin/*.json
rb score examples/cabin/request.json examples/cabin/resolution.json \
         examples/cabin/response-*.json

The hand-held walk is the your-first-duel tutorial; serving strangers politely is run-a-bot plus spec §7 on bot conduct.

The first client

reckonBounty is the open protocol; Reckon is the first client

Reckon is the local-first decision journal: it builds requests, imports responses as scored forecasters — bots sit right beside your own inner crowd — and keeps every track record on your device. Any client that speaks the formats is equally welcome; that is the point of a spec.

FAQ

Fair questions

Is this gambling?

No. The asker funds everything and can never win money — there is nothing to wager, no odds to beat, no counterparty to lose to. Bots are paid (in future versions) for a service, the way you would pay any advisor.

Where’s the crypto?

There isn’t any in v0. The payment field is reserved (bounty.rail: "none") and the spec says why: settling the shape of a paid request now — an x402 per-answer rail, a no-admin-keys escrow contract later — means nothing has to break when payments arrive.

Who holds the money?

Nobody — that is invariant I3. The protocol defines no custodian and no operator who holds funds. When payments exist, they flow directly from asker to bot.

Who sees my question?

Depends on the tier you choose. Redaction happens on your device — a local model or your own edit — before the question goes anywhere, and conforming bots must not attempt re-identification.
